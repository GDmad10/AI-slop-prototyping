# Hosting this depiction on your repo
1. Copy `ghostipa-tweak/depiction/` to your repo as `depic/com.ghostipa.tweak/`.
   - `index.html` (this page)
   - `icon.png` — 120px+ PNG of the tweak logo (drop the file in next to index.html)
2. In the repo's Packages file (or `control`), set:
     Depiction: https://YOUR-REPO/depic/com.ghostipa.tweak/
     Icon: https://YOUR-REPO/depic/com.ghostipa.tweak/icon.png
   Cydia renders Depiction; Sileo/Zebra use both (Icon = list thumbnail).
3. Sizes: icon.png ≥ 120×120 (240×240 ideal, @2x). Keep the rounded-rectangle
   artwork full-bleed — Cydia/Sileo mask it themselves. Do NOT pre-round.
